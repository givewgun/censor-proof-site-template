## Executive Summary

> **Note:** Every entity, contract number, and figure in this case is **100% fictional** and exists solely to demonstrate how to structure a factual, neutral, legally defensible public-interest dossier.

Between January and September 2025, the fictional **Klong-Sai Municipality** awarded three consecutive emergency flood-drainage culvert contracts totaling **142.5 million THB** without competitive e-bidding. Cross-checking public procurement announcements against sanitized bid records (`EVD-002`) indicates unit pricing averaging **2.4x** the regional Comptroller General reference price.

## Key Verifiable Findings

1. **Emergency-Waiver Splitting:** A single contiguous 4.2 km canal project was split into three separate tenders scheduled 11 days apart, keeping each tranche below the mandatory public-hearing threshold.
2. **Repeated Winning Consortium:** Two nominally separate winning bidders shared identical technical specification appendices and unit-rate rounding errors (`EVD-001`).
3. **Public-Interest Impact:** Downstream residential neighborhoods experienced severe seasonal flooding despite 100% payment disbursement recorded as "completed."

## Editorial & Sanitization Methodology

- All figures were corroborated against publicly filed municipal budget tables prior to release.
- Source files were reconstructed into plain UTF-8 text and CSV (`EVD-001`, `EVD-002`) to eliminate document metadata, printer tracking patterns, and author stylometry.
- Neutral, factual tone is maintained throughout; no unverified allegations or private personal data are included.
