## Overview

Because IPFS CIDs and BitTorrent archives are **immutable**, factual corrections cannot be made silently. This fictional sample demonstrates how a transparent, signed correction notice is embedded directly into the dossier and top-level release manifest.

## Summary of Audited Ledger (`EVD-003`)

- Audited weighbridge logs across four transfer stations showed billed tonnage exceeding registered truck fleet maximum payload capacity by **31%** during Q2 2025.
- Upon independent review, Annex B's conversion footnote was clarified to report **metric tons** consistently across all quarters (`CORR-2026-001`), without changing the underlying 31% overbilling discrepancy.
